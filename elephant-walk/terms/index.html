What texts will I get?

Only updates about a pickup order you placed with us: when the kitchen has accepted it, and when it's ready. If you change your order, we'll let you know the kitchen has the updated one.

No ads, offers or promotions. Ever.

How do I sign up?

  • Ordering by phone. After you place your order, our ordering assistant asks if we can text you updates about it. You only get texts if you say yes.
  • Ordering by text. If you text us to place an order, we'll reply by text about that order.

You don't have to agree to texts to place an order.

How often, and what does it cost?

Usually two texts per order, a few more if you change your order. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How do I stop?

Reply STOP to any of our texts. You'll get one text confirming you're unsubscribed, and then nothing more.

How do I get help?

Reply HELP to any of our texts, or call us at (513) 526-1555.

Carriers

Mobile carriers aren't responsible for texts that arrive late or don't arrive.

Your privacy

We never sell, rent or share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with anyone for marketing. Our privacy policy has the details.
